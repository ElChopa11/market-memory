---

## Claim under test (falsifiable)

On the Phase-1 monitor panel, for each instrument with interpretable sample (regime bucket n ≥ 100):

> Long 1R:2R bracket hit rate (R = 1×ATR20, first touch within 10 bars) under **trend-up** exceeds the same instrument’s **unconditional** long hit rate by a material margin after the flat pessimistic cost shift.

Null we accept if trend-up hit ≈ unconditional hit (within noise), or is lower. A fair 1:2 coin-flip is ~33.3% as a **descriptive mixture** only — **not** a strategy hurdle. Pooled unconditional long (voids excluded) is reported as a mixture weight, not a candidate null. Per-instrument unconditional brackets are the hurdle.

---

## Sample caveats (state plainly)

1. **Equity signals start ~2025-07-09.** Polygon’s 2-year free-tier history cap (~501 daily bars) means SMA200 is only available after ~200 bars → equity regime signals begin around **2025-07-09**. Every equity base rate in the source file is **PROVISIONAL** (one year of usable signals).
2. **One regime for equities.** That window is a single post-2025 stretch — not a multi-regime stress test. Do not generalise equity Δ-hit to “all markets.”
3. **Do-not-interpret buckets (n < 100) among included names:** trend-down **7/22**, chop **9/22** (cite Phase-1 regime tables). Those buckets are reported in the source file and are **not** used for interpretation here.
4. Continuity voids (QQQ, SPCX, NVDA, BB, BMNR, MRNA, STRC) are out of the pooled bracket. Not re-introduced here.
5. Bracket is always-long / always-short 1R:2R with a flat cost shift — not a strategy backtest, not a size, not a scan gate.

## What this means for C-001 / C-002 / C-003

All three candidates assume a **trend-permission** gate (price vs SMA200 / uptrend context) carries information before their specific entry mechanics fire.

| Candidate | Shared premise | Implication of this study |
|---|---|---|
| C-001 supply/demand zone | Revisit + confirm inside an uptrend / HTF structure | Permission filter alone does not raise bracket hit. C-001 must show **incremental** edge vs the instrument’s own **unconditional** bracket, and vs that instrument’s own **trend-up** bracket — not vs pooled 33.3%. |
| C-002 triple RSI MR | Three RSIs oversold **and** price > SMA200 with SMA50 rising | Macro filter is doing most of the marketing in the source claim. On this sample the macro filter is near-null / harmful. C-002’s RSI stack must beat **trend-up alone** and beat single-RSI / SMA20-dip benchmarks (intake rule 3). |
| C-003 second-entry pullback | Pullback continuation in trend | Same: second-entry must beat first-entry **and** beat trend-up unconditional bracket on that name. |

**Not a rejection of C-001/002/003.** They remain `INTAKE_ONLY` / HYPOTHESIS. **Is** a rejection of “gate 1 (trend permission) is an edge by itself.” Any later study that uses that gate without beating the instrument’s **own unconditional** bracket **and** its own trend-up base rate is measuring decoration. IC Gate 1 remains **FAIL**.

Cross-candidate overlap (intake rule 8) stays mandatory before any promotion — all three are dip-in-uptrend variants and will co-fire.

## VVVUSD (~47.3% trend-up, n=200)

| Slice | n | long gross hit | long net hit | 1b mean | 10b mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Unconditional | 579 bracket / 600 bars | 43.3% | 43.5% | +0.527% | +5.702% |
| Trend-up | 200 | **47.3%** | **47.4%** | +1.237% | +8.096% |
| Trend-down | 117 | 27.5% | 28.0% | −0.435% | −3.341% |

**Interpretation: drift capture in a single run-up, not a portable filter effect.**

1. Unconditional VVV already prints **43.3%** long hit — far above *that name's* own sample, and also above the pooled ~33% **descriptive mixture** — before any trend gate. The “edge” is mostly **in the name’s sample**, not in the filter. Do not use pooled 33% as the hurdle.
2. Trend-up window is short and late: **2025-08-17 → 2026-09-19** (n=200). One crypto run-up regime.
3. Trend-down on the same name collapses to **27.5%** hit with negative forward means — consistent with a trending tape, not with a mean-reversion permission skill.
4. Vol is extreme (vol20 mean ~140% ann in unconditional stats). High hit on 1R:2R in a melt-up is the expected shape of **short-vol / trend-following payoff on a runaway name**, not evidence the SMA200+SMA50-rising rule generalises.
5. Δ hit vs uncond is only **+4.1%** on an already-elevated base. Not a discovery; not a promotion; not a size.

**Do not** treat VVVUSD as validation of the permission filter for the candidate queue.

## Verdict

**FAIL** — trend-permission filter does not carry standalone edge on the Phase-1 sample.

- Expected outcome; **publish**.
- C-001 / C-002 / C-003 remain `INTAKE_ONLY`. Do not promote. Do not reject on this file alone.
- Later candidate studies must beat **that instrument's own unconditional** 1R:2R bracket (and, if they use a trend-up permission filter, **that instrument's own trend-up** bracket), record params before first run, and stop with PASS | FAIL | INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE (intake rules 10–12). The pooled ~33% is a descriptive mixture, not a hurdle. IC Gate 1 remains FAIL.
- Equity rows stay provisional until history extends past the Polygon 2y free-tier SMA200 start (~2025-07-09).

What would make this result spurious: a multi-regime equity history that reverses the TSLA/HOOD/AMD pattern; or a definition of “trend-up” other than close>SMA200 ∧ SMA50 rising (that would be a new candidate version, not a silent retune).
